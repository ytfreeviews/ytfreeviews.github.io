FacebookVelocity Multi-Panel Video & Reel Viewer — Multi-Slot Layout Previewer

The FacebookVelocity Multi-Panel Video & Reel Viewer is a browser-based preview tool built for social media managers, editors, and page creators who work with Facebook Watch videos, Live broadcasts, and vertical Reels. Instead of juggling dozens of separate browser tabs, you can load public Facebook video embeds into a single responsive grid to check framing, pacing, and readability side by side—with no login or software download required.

Because Facebook distributes both horizontal 16:9 clips and vertical 9:16 Reels across mobile feeds and desktop screens, visual details like burned-in captions, lower-third titles, and crop margins can look very different depending on screen width. Reviewing multiple slots together makes it quick to spot overlapping UI elements or formatting issues before publishing or promoting a post.
