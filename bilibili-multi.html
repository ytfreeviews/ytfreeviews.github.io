Bilibili Multi-Viewer — Side-by-Side Workspace for Creators & Stream Viewers

The Bilibili Multi-Viewer Tool is a lightweight browser grid designed for video editors, animators, gaming commentators, and Bilibili viewers who want to watch or compare multiple video slots in a single window. Instead of switching back and forth between heavy browser tabs, you can paste a Bilibili video URL or BV ID and preview embedded 16:9 players side by side in an organized layout.

From anime breakdowns and tech hardware reviews to gameplay VODs, Bilibili creators rely on clean pacing, clear subtitle timing, and sharp visual composition. Having multiple scaled embeds visible together makes it simple to check how on-screen text and video overlays read across different desktop and tablet window sizes.

📖 How to Use the Bilibili Multi-Panel Grid Tool

01

Copy Link or BV ID

Open any public Bilibili video and copy either the full page URL or its unique BV identifier (for example, BV1GJ411x7h7).

02

Paste Into Field

Enter the Bilibili link or BV/av ID into the input box at the top of the workspace.

03

Set Slot Count

Choose how many embedded video panels you want to open simultaneously (from 1 up to 20 slots).

04

Pick Column Layout

Select Auto or a fixed 1 to 5 column grid from the dropdown to fit your monitor resolution.

05

Launch Workspace

Click the Launch Workspace button to render the embedded Bilibili players side by side.

06

Clear & Switch

Whenever you want to inspect another video or adjust column density, click Clear All to reset the grid.
