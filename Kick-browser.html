How to Use the Kick Multi-Browser Stream Viewer

Opening several Kick broadcasts across separate browser tabs quickly turns into clutter, overlapping audio, and high memory use. The Kick Multi-Browser Stream Viewer organizes public Kick.com embeds inside a single clean grid so streamers, moderators, and esports viewers can follow multiple channels side by side.

  • Enter Channel Username or URL: Paste a public Kick channel URL (such as https://kick.com/username) or type the streamer's handle directly into the top input box.
  • Choose Panel Count: Pick how many stream boxes to load at once (from 1 up to 20 panels, depending on your computer's CPU and internet speed).
  • Pick a Grid Layout: Select 1, 2, 3, or 4 fixed columns—or leave Auto enabled so the grid adapts cleanly to your display width.
  • Launch Streams: Click Launch Streams to render muted Kick players side by side so you can unmute whichever feed you want to hear.

Practical Features for Viewers & Streamers

Everything runs locally inside your browser tab with standard Kick player embeds—keeping setup fast and straightforward.

  • Local Browser Execution: Your entered channel names and layout choices stay inside your browser session with no extra proxies or external logins.
