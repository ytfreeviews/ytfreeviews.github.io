5 Practical Habits for Better Reel Copy

1. Lead with the Outcome: Put the main takeaway or curiosity hook in the first 60–80 characters before Instagram truncates the text with "...more".
2. Break Up Long Lines: Short 1–2 sentence paragraphs and clean spacing are far easier to skim on a phone screen than dense blocks of text.
3. Use Natural Search Words: Instagram search indexes words inside your caption, so include the exact topic viewers might type.
4. Ask a Specific Question: Prompts like "Which setup do you prefer—A or B?" get more natural replies than generic "Comment below!" requests.
5. Pick Focused Hashtags: Stick to 3–5 accurate tags describing the topic and format rather than pasting dozens of unrelated trending tags.

Edit Before You Paste
