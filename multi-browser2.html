How Multi Browser 2.0 Works for Side-by-Side Comparison

Multi-URL Round-Robin Mode: Unlike Version 1.0 (which repeats one video across every tile), Multi Browser 2.0 accepts up to four separate YouTube URLs and rotates them evenly across your chosen grid. Keep audio muted when launching several windows at once, and check YouTube Studio for official channel analytics (stats can take 24–48 hours to settle).

YouTube Multi Browser 2.0 makes it simple to review an entire mini-series, A/B compare two different edits, or study pacing across four public videos in one browser tab. Built purely with standard YouTube iframe embeds, it requires no login and works on desktop and tablet screens alike. Please use embedded viewers responsibly and in compliance with YouTube's platform policies.

Quick Setup Steps

  • Step 1: Copy between one and four YouTube Video or Shorts URLs.
  • Step 2: Paste your primary link into Box #1 and any comparison links into Boxes #2–#4.
  • Step 3: Choose the total number of player tiles (for instance, 4 tiles for a 1-to-1 comparison or 8 tiles for dual rows).
  • Step 4: Pick muted or sound-enabled playback and your preferred autoplay toggle.
  • Step 5: Click Generate Players to distribute your selected videos across the grid.

Why Creators Compare Up to Four Videos Together

Viewing episodes of a playlist side by side quickly highlights inconsistencies—such as mismatched audio loudness, shifting lower-third graphics, or opening hooks that drag too long compared to stronger uploads on your channel.
