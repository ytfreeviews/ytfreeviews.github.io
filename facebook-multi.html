Preview Facebook Videos & Reels Side by Side

The Facebook Video Multi Viewer lets creators, editors, and page managers inspect public Facebook Watch clips, Live streams, and vertical Reels across multiple embedded frames at once. Instead of opening a dozen tabs just to check how a video scales across desktop and mobile column widths, paste your link, select the window count, and preview playback right in your browser without any downloads or account sign-in.

Quick Usage Notes

  • Public Links Only: Paste the full URL of a public Facebook video or Reel (private or friends-only posts will not load inside external embeds).
  • Keep Slot Counts Reasonable: Start with 2 to 6 windows on mobile or mid-range laptops for smooth playback without slowing down your browser.
